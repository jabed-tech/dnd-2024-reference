# Content & data accuracy audit

Scope: `src/data/*.json` compared against `source/extracted/*.txt` and, where the extraction looked suspect, the PDF itself (`source/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf`, read with PyMuPDF). Generators: `scripts/*.py`. `npm run validate` passes (157 glossary, 38 weapons / 10 properties / 8 masteries, 145 equipment). It does not catch any of the problems below.

## What checks out (no finding)

- **Glossary completeness:** the PDF has exactly 155 entry headings (GillSans-SemiBold 12pt, pp. 176–191), and all 155 are in `glossary.json`. No duplicate slugs or titles. No dangling `seeAlso` slugs, self-references or duplicate `seeAlso` items.
- **Glossary tables:** every cell in all 9 hand-authored tables (Breaking Objects ×2, Carrying Capacity, Damage Types, Dehydration, Malnutrition, Influence, Search, Study) exists in the source, and the rows read correctly.
- **Glossary lead-ins:** all 99 bold-italic lead-ins in the PDF are `<strong>` in the JSON. The 11 extra `<strong>` items (Long Rest, Short Rest, Truesight sub-items) are Cambria-Bold in the PDF, so they are also right.
- **Body text:** every sentence of 12 characters or more in the glossary, combat-rules, equipment, properties and masteries bodies appears in the extracted source. No stray page headers or footers, no `<<<PAGE` markers, no unbalanced HTML, no paragraph that starts lower-case (a split mid-sentence).
- **Equipment:** all 82 Adventuring Gear rows match the SRD gear table by name, weight and cost. All 13 armour, 25 tools (17 artisan + 8 other), 8 mounts, 10 tack/drawn items and 7 ships are present. Every tool and armour `stats` value appears in the source.
- **Weapons:** all 38 are present. Damage, properties, mastery, weight and cost match the SRD. Every property and mastery slug resolves.
- **`costCp` / `weightLb`:** these match the `cost` / `weight` strings for every equipment item and weapon. The one exception is Sling (finding L1).
- **Spelling:** no British spellings ("Armour" and so on) in the data. Rules text keeps SRD US spelling throughout.

## High

### H1. `equipment-intro.json` is flattened: headings and lead-ins are lost, a table caption leaks in, and the Vehicles intro contains a different, truncated section
- **Where:** `src/data/equipment-intro.json:2` (armor), `:3` (tools), `:5` (mounts), `:6` (vehicles). Generated by `scripts/build_equipment.py` `parse_armor()` (l.272), `parse_tools()` (l.333), `parse_mounts()` (l.553, l.569) and `to_html()`/`join_wrapped()` (l.159–197). The root cause is in `scripts/extract_srd.py` `marked_page_text()`.
- **What's wrong:**
  1. **Lead-ins and headings are lost.** Each intro is one wall-of-text `<p>`. Lead-ins such as `Category.`, `Strength.`, `Ability.` and `Craft.` are not bold, and sub-headings run straight into the sentence after them: "…Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks. **Armor Training Anyone can don armor**…", "**Mounts and Cargo An animal pulling**…", "**Speed A ship sailing**…", "**Shield You gain**…".
  2. **A table caption leaks into the Armor intro.** It ends with a stray "**… one Shield at a time. Armor**".
  3. **The Vehicles intro swallows the next section.** It runs on past Ship Repair into **Lifestyle Expenses** and **Food, Drink, and Lodging**, with the price table flattened into prose ("Food, Drink, and Lodging Item Cost Ale (mug) 4 CP Bread (loaf) 2 CP … Item Cost Comfortable 8 SP …"). Because extraction stops at p.101, that table is also **cut off at "Meal … Modest 1 SP"**. The missing rows are Meal Comfortable 2 SP / Wealthy 3 SP / Aristocratic 6 SP and Wine Common 2 SP / Fine 10 GP (PDF p.102).
- **Why it matters:** these are the first text a user reads on each equipment section, and they are hard to read. Lifestyle prices show up under "Vehicles", and a price table is presented incomplete as if it were whole. The rules text itself is otherwise correct.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - `extract_srd.py` only marks `Cambria-BoldItalic`. The equipment intros use `Cambria-Bold` for lead-ins (p.89, p.92) and `GillSans-SemiBold` for sub-headings ("Armor Training", "Mounts and Cargo", "Speed", "Crew" and so on). Mark Cambria-Bold runs as `**…**` too, and mark GillSans-SemiBold heading lines with a prefix such as `## `.
  - In `to_html()`, render `## ` lines as `<h3>` and start a new paragraph at each `**` lead-in, not only on tab indents.
  - In `parse_armor()`, slice the intro to `start - 2` (or drop a trailing line equal to the caption) so the "Armor" caption is excluded.
  - In `parse_mounts()`, end `vehicles_intro` at the "Lifestyle Expenses" heading. Either drop Lifestyle and Food/Lodging, or extend extraction to p.102 and emit them as their own section with a real `<table>`.
  - Add a validator rule: an intro must not end with a bare Title-Case word, and must contain no "Item Cost" run.
- **Severity:** high

## Medium

### M1. 18 summaries cut off mid-sentence with "…"; two are cut mid-word
- **Where:**
  - glossary (11): `ally` (glossary.json:87), `damage-threshold` (:508), `dodge-action` (:673), `high-jump` (:904), `hit-point-dice` (:911), `magic-action` (:1125), `simultaneous-effects` (:1439), `spellcasting-focus` (:1511), `study-action` (:1580), `target` (:1652), `tremorsense` (:1692)
  - properties.json: `heavy` (:15), `loading` (:27)
  - masteries.json: `cleave` (:3), `topple` (:39), `vex` (:45)
  - equipment.json: `chain` (:541) "…DC 13 Strength (**At…**", and `manacles` (:1509) "…if you succeed on **a D…**"
- **What's wrong:**
  - `build_glossary.py first_sentence()` (l.536) hard-caps the first sentence at 160 characters and appends "…".
  - `build_equipment.py summarise()` (l.210) does `text[:197] + "…"` without respecting word boundaries.
  - properties.json and masteries.json have no generator, but follow the same 160-character pattern.
- **Why it matters:**
  - Summaries are shown on index cards (`EntryCard`, equipment table `e-summary`).
  - They are used as the page `<meta description>` (`AppShell description={entry.summary}`).
  - They are indexed for search.
  - A truncated rule ("Disadvantage if you…") can be misleading, and "(At…" / "a D…" look broken.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Raise the cap to about 250 characters, or cut at the last `,`/`;`/`—` before the cap. The card already clamps to 2 lines with CSS (`-webkit-line-clamp`), so the data does not need to be short.
  - In `summarise()`, cut with `rsplit(" ", 1)[0]`, as the glossary already does.
  - Add a validator warning for summaries ending in "…".
- **Severity:** med

### M2. Line-break hyphens dropped from real compounds: "10-footsquare", "15foot"
- **Where:** equipment.json `ball-bearings` (:153 body and summary context: "cover a level, 10-footsquare area") and `lamp` (:1346 body: "Bright Light in a 15foot radius"). Origin: `scripts/extract_srd.py` l.56 `re.sub(r"-\n(?=[a-z])", "", text)`.
- **What's wrong:** the de-hyphenation regex removes every line-end hyphen that comes before a lower-case letter. That includes hyphens in compounds: PDF p.96 has "10-foot-⏎square" and p.98 has "15-⏎foot". I checked all 282 line-end hyphens in the extracted page ranges, and these are the only two that broke.
- **Why it matters:** these are visible typos in rules text. "15foot" is also the kind of thing that defeats search for "15-foot".
- **Suggested fix:** in `extract_srd.py`, keep the hyphen when the fragment before it contains a digit or another hyphen. For example, first run `re.sub(r"(\b[\w]*[\d-][\w-]*)-\n(?=[a-z])", r"\1-", text)`, then apply the general rule. "hand-⏎held" and "hand-⏎written" (p.182/p.184) are ambiguous, but the SRD prints "handheld"/"handwritten", so the current output is right. Add a validator regex for `\d+[a-z]{3,}` and `-[a-z]+square`.
- **Severity:** med

### M3. Oil summary merges two paragraphs: "as detailed below.Dousing a Creature or an Object."
- **Where:** equipment.json `oil` (:1690). Origin: `build_equipment.py summarise()` l.212, `re.sub(r"<[^>]+>", "", html)`.
- **What's wrong:** tags are stripped with no space, so `</p><p><strong>` joins "below." to "Dousing". The sentence regex `[.!?](?:\s|$)` then does not stop at "below.", and the summary runs on into the next lead-in.
- **Why it matters:** the summary is visibly broken in the gear table and in the meta description. The same bug will hit any future item whose first paragraph is followed by a lead-in.
- **Suggested fix:** replace tags with `" "` (as `stripHtml` in `src/lib/data.ts` and the glossary builder already do), then collapse whitespace.
- **Severity:** med

### M4. Spurious `seeAlso` links from case-sensitive, sentence-start matches (about 27), especially `attack-action` on 7 conditions
- **Where:** glossary.json `seeAlso` of:
  - `blinded-condition` (:183), `invisible-condition` (:1039), `paralyzed-condition` (:1209), `petrified-condition` (:1245), `restrained-condition` (:1360), `stunned-condition` (:1591), `unconscious-condition` (:1725). All of these link to **`attack-action`** only because a sentence starts "Attack rolls against you…".
  - `concentration` (:363), `improvised-weapons` (:972), `unarmed-strike` (:1711) and `stat-block` (:1542) link to `damage`/`weapon`/`saving-throw`/`attack-action` only through sentence-initial "Damage…", "Weapon…" or "Saving Throw Effect Notation".
  - `invisible-condition` links to `surprise` the same way.
  - `carrying-capacity` (:300) links to `creature`, and `breaking-objects` (:232) links to `size`, from table headers.
  - combat-rules.json `underwater-combat` (:21) lists `attack-roll` and `damage-types`, but neither term appears in its body.
- **What's wrong:** `build_glossary.py scan_body_mentions()` (l.592) matches each glossary title as a case-sensitive whole word anywhere in the plain text, including table header cells and the first word of a sentence. "Attack" (the title of Attack [Action]) therefore matches "Attack rolls". Meanwhile the correct target, `attack-roll`, is often missing, because "attack rolls" is lower-case mid-sentence. The site's `linkTerms()` (`src/lib/data.ts` l.206) uses the same case-sensitive rule, so it makes the same wrong inline link in those bodies.
- **Why it matters:** the related-rules column sends readers from "Blinded" to the Attack *action* rather than *Attack Roll*. The links look authoritative but are noise.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - In `scan_body_mentions()`, try the longest titles first and consume the matched span, so "Attack Roll(s)" wins over "Attack".
  - Ignore matches inside `<th>`/`<td>` header rows.
  - Treat a sentence-initial match as a mention only if the following word does not extend it into a longer known term ("Attack rolls" should go to `attack-roll`).
  - Apply the same longest-match idea case-insensitively for multi-word titles.
  - For `build_combat_rules.py`, derive `seeAlso` from the body the same way, rather than from a hand list.
- **Severity:** med

### M5. Tack, saddles, feed and stabling are filed as `kind: "vehicle"`
- **Where:** equipment.json `saddle-exotic` (:2124), `saddle-military` (:2138), `saddle-riding` (:2152), `feed-per-day` (:891), `stabling-per-day` (:2429). Also carriage, cart, chariot, sled and wagon (group "Tack, Harness, and Drawn Vehicles"). Origin: `build_equipment.py parse_mounts()` l.560–565 passes `"vehicle"` for the whole Tack table.
- **What's wrong:** saddles, feed and stabling are not vehicles. The UI's "Vehicles" filter (`EQUIPMENT_KINDS` in data.ts) lists them next to ships, under the Vehicles intro, which says nothing about them. The rules that explain saddles ("Saddles", "Barding", "Mounts and Cargo") are in the **mounts** intro.
- **Why it matters:** users looking for a Military Saddle under Mounts won't find it. Kind and intro text don't line up.
- **Suggested fix:** emit the Tack table with `kind: "mount"`. Alternatively, add a `tack` kind, or split it: saddles, feed and stabling as `mount`, drawn vehicles as `vehicle`. Update `EquipmentKind` and the validator to match.
- **Severity:** med

### M6. SRD Equipment content missing or out of range; intro text points to sections the site doesn't have
- **Where:** the extraction range in `scripts/extract_srd.py` l.73 (`extract(doc, 89, 101, "equipment.txt")`); `equipment-intro.json:3` and `:6`; `weapons-intro.json`.
- **What's wrong:** the following SRD content on the extracted pages, or the next page, is not in any JSON file:
  - **Coins / Coin Values table** (p.89: CP 1/100, SP 1/10, EP 1/2, GP 1, PP 10; 50 coins = 1 lb.). The site uses CP/SP/GP everywhere and has `costCp` conversions, but never shows the table.
  - **Selling Equipment** (p.89: items sell for half their cost).
  - **Weapon Proficiency** sidebar (p.89). This is flagged as deliberately omitted in `source/reports/weapons-diff.md`.
  - **Food, Drink, and Lodging** (only part of it, and in the wrong place; see H1), **Hirelings** and **Spellcasting Services** (p.102).
  - **Crafting Nonmagical Items / Brewing Potions of Healing / Scribing Spell Scrolls** (p.103–104). The tools intro (`equipment-intro.json:3`) tells readers to see these "later in 'Equipment'", and the vehicles intro refers to "Hirelings" later in "Equipment". Neither target exists on the site.
  - Also not imported, from `combat.txt` (pp.15–16): "Ranged Attacks in Close Combat", the Cover table, "Damage against Multiple Targets" and "Half Damage". These are lower priority, because most have glossary equivalents.
- **Why it matters:** the text leads readers to sections that don't exist. Coin conversion and crafting are among the most-asked rules questions about tools and gear.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Extend the equipment extraction to p.104.
  - Add these as intro blocks with real tables, or as a small `services` kind (Hirelings, Spellcasting, Lifestyle, Food/Lodging rows also fit `equipment.json` with `cost`/`costCp`).
  - Add Coins and Weapon Proficiency to the weapons/equipment intros.
  - Or, if they stay out of scope, rewrite or strip the "later in 'Equipment'" pointers.
- **Severity:** med

### M7. Weapons data has no generator, and the glossary build depends on the legacy site and hand-written tables
- **Where:** `src/data/weapons.json`, `properties.json`, `masteries.json`, `weapons-intro.json`. No script in `scripts/` writes them (`grep` finds only `validate_weapons.mjs`), and the README's "Regenerating the data" lists none. `scripts/build_glossary.py` l.73–77 reads `source/legacy/dnd_2024.html` and asserts exactly 155 legacy titles. It uses that list to decide which headings are real. `TABLE_BODIES` (9 tables) is hand-authored HTML inside the script.
- **What's wrong:** the README says the PDF is the source of truth, but the four weapons files can't be regenerated, so M1-style fixes have to be made by hand. `weapons.txt` is also known to be mis-ordered (see weapons-diff.md). The glossary set is gated by the old site: any SRD heading not on the legacy site would be silently dropped. Today the PDF has exactly 155 headings, so nothing is lost, but the dependency is fragile.
- **Why it matters:** fixes and SRD updates won't propagate the same way to every file. There's a hidden dependency on the site this project replaces.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Add `scripts/build_weapons.py`, parsing pp.89–92 with the PyMuPDF dict API (font-based headings, as in the glossary heading check).
  - In `build_glossary.py`, detect headings by font: GillSans-SemiBold 12pt gives exactly 155 on pp.176–191. Remove the legacy HTML dependency.
  - Keep `TABLE_BODIES`, but add a check that every cell exists in the source text, as this audit did.
- **Severity:** med

## Low

### L1. Sling `weightLb` is `0` but its weight is "—"; equipment uses `null` for "—"
- **Where:** weapons.json `sling` (:335, `"weight": "—", "weightLb": 0` at l.355–356). `validate_weapons.mjs` l.130 requires a number.
- **Why it matters:** the conventions differ between files (equipment uses `null` for "—"). Sorting by weight treats a weightless Sling as 0 lb, but sorts "—" equipment separately.
- **Suggested fix:** use `null` and let `validate_weapons.mjs` accept `null` when `weight` is "—", matching `validate_equipment.mjs`.
- **Severity:** low

### L2. A line-end em dash is joined with a space: "wall— is", "GM— whose"
- **Where:** glossary.json `area-of-effect` (:98 body, "an obstruction—such as a wall— is between") and `simultaneous-effects` (:1442–1443, both summary and body, "player or GM— whose").
- **What's wrong:** `build_glossary.py join_hyphen()` (l.191) joins every wrapped line with `" "`, even when the previous line ends in "—".
- **Suggested fix:** if `acc.endswith("—")`, join without a space. The equipment builder's `join_wrapped()` (l.159) has the same weakness, though it isn't triggered today.
- **Severity:** low

### L3. Templated or weak summaries for items without prose (about 45)
- **Where:**
  - 25 tools, e.g. `gaming-set` (equipment.json:981) "Wisdom tool. Discern whether someone is cheating (DC 10), or win the game (DC 20)". It has no final full stop.
  - 18 mounts and tack items, e.g. `camel` (:398) "Mounts and Other Animals. 50 GP." and `carriage` (:458) "Tack, Harness, and Drawn Vehicles. 100 GP." These repeat the group and the cost column.
  - `potion-of-healing` (:1909) "This potion is a magic item."
  - `spell-scroll-cantrip` (:2342) and `spell-scroll-level-1` (:2356) have identical summary and body.
  - Armour: "Shield. AC +2."
- **Why it matters:** cards and meta descriptions add no information, and the search corpus fills with group names.
- **Suggested fix:** in `build_equipment.py add(... fallback=...)`, build fallbacks from the stats, for example "Mount; carries 450 lb.", "Tack; 600 lb.", "Light armor, AC 11 + Dex, Disadvantage on Stealth". End tool fallbacks with ".". For the Potion of Healing, use the second sentence, or skip any sentence under about 40 characters.
- **Severity:** low

### L4. Item names don't match each other across lists
- **Where:**
  - Tool `Craft` stats use natural order — "Glass Bottle", "Iron Pot", "Iron Spikes", "Bullseye Lantern", "Hooded Lantern", "Basic Poison", "Firearm Bullets", "Sling Bullets" (e.g. `smiths-tools`, `tinkers-tools`, `poisoners-kit`). The item names are inverted: "Bottle, Glass", "Pot, Iron", "Lantern, Bullseye", "Poison, Basic".
  - Weapons give ammo as singular "Bolt", "Arrow", "Bullet" and "Needle" (weapons.json `properties[].ammo`). The Ammunition table uses "Bolts", "Arrows", "Bullets, Firearm", "Bullets, Sling" and "Needles". Sling and Musket both say just "Bullet".
  - "Feed per day" and "Stabling per day" (:891, :2429) are lower-case phrases, unlike the inverted "Saddle, Exotic" pattern.
- **Why it matters:** the SRD itself is inconsistent here, but it stops Craft lists and ammo types from being cross-linked to item pages, and makes search less predictable.
- **Suggested fix:** keep the SRD display text, but add an alias map in `build_equipment.py` (natural order → slug), so Craft and ammo values can be emitted as `{text, slug}` and linked. Name the tack rows "Feed (per day)" and "Stabling (per day)", or keep them as they are and add aliases.
- **Severity:** low

### L5. Fractions are written two ways in weights and speeds

- **Severity:** low

### L6. Glossary tables have no `<caption>`; equipment tables do

## Suggested validator additions

These would have caught most of the findings above:

1. Fail on a `summary` ending in "…", and on any `[a-z][.!?][A-Z]` run in summaries (M1, M3).
2. Fail on `\d+[a-z]{3,}`, `—\s` / `\s—` and `\*\*` in any string (M2, L2).
3. Check that intros do not end with a bare title-case word and contain no "Item Cost" sequences (H1).
4. Warn when a `seeAlso` target's title appears in the body only at the start of a sentence (M4).
5. Check that weapons' `weightLb` is `null` exactly when `weight` is "—" (L1).
